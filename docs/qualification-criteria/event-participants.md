# Qualification criteria: event participants

Draft 1. To be reviewed and updated by the Innovista team. Duplicate this document for each event
and fill in the event details.

## Summary

We are looking for companies taking part in a named event (exhibitors, sponsors, speakers and, where
known, attendees) whose business matches what the client offers, so that they can be contacted before,
during or after the event. Participation is the reason to contact them now; the business match is the
reason they are worth contacting.

Event: [name, city, dates, website]
Offer: [what the client wants to discuss with participants]

## Who to contact

In this order, when found on the company's own website or in the imported list:

1. The person named as exhibitor or speaker contact in the imported event list.
2. Head of sales, business development or partnerships.
3. Marketing manager or events manager.
4. Founder or managing director, for companies under 50 people.

## Criteria

Each criterion is judged Meets, Does not meet or Unable to verify, with the evidence quoted.

1. **Takes part in the event.** Evidence: the event name and role (exhibitor, sponsor, speaker,
   attendee) in the imported lead data, or an announcement of the participation on the company's own
   website. This criterion must be met for the lead to qualify.
2. **Participation role.** Exhibitor or sponsor is the strongest; speaker next; attendee weakest.
   Evidence: the role column in the imported list.
3. **Business matches the offer.** Evidence: the company's products or services on its own website,
   compared against the offer described above.
4. **Right size and region.** Evidence: size and location from the lead record or the company's own
   site, against the client's target range [to be filled in].
5. **Contact reachable in time.** A named person or a sales or events address is available, and the
   event has not ended more than 60 days ago.

## Exclusions

A lead matching any of these is Not a target, whatever else it scores.

1. **The event organiser, venue and service suppliers** (stand builders, caterers, media partners).
2. **Direct competitors of the client** exhibiting at the same event.
3. **Participation that cannot be confirmed** from the imported list or the company's own site.
4. **Events that ended more than 90 days ago**, unless the client asks for follow-up campaigns.

## Evidence the system can check

Criterion 1 and 2 depend on the imported list: import the event's exhibitor, sponsor or speaker list
as the leads, with columns for the event name and the participation role. The research pass cannot
read the event's website. Criteria 3 to 5 can be verified from the company's own website.

## What the fit score means here

- 80 to 100: confirmed exhibitor or sponsor, strong business match, reachable contact. Call before
  the event to book a meeting at the stand.
- 70 to 79: confirmed participant with a good match but an unverified detail. Email.
- 50 to 69: confirmed participant, unclear match. Review with the client.
- Below 50: participation unconfirmed or no match. Archive.

## Open questions for the client

1. Which events, and which participation roles matter?
2. What exactly should the conversation at the event be about?
3. Should outreach happen before the event (to book meetings), after it (follow-up), or both?
