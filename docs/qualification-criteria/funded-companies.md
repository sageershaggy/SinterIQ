# Qualification criteria: companies that have received significant investment

Draft 1. To be reviewed and updated by the Innovista team.

## Summary

We are looking for companies that have recently raised significant investment, because new funding
comes with plans to hire, build and buy. The right lead announced a funding round in the last 12
months, is in a sector the client serves, and states what the money is for in a way that matches the
client's offer.

## Who to contact

In this order, when found on the company's own website:

1. Founder or chief executive, for companies under 100 people.
2. Chief operating officer or chief financial officer.
3. The head of the function the funding is for (for example the CTO when the round is for product
   development, the head of sales when it is for expansion).
4. Head of partnerships or business development.

## Criteria

Each criterion is judged Meets, Does not meet or Unable to verify, with the evidence quoted.

1. **Raised a funding round in the last 12 months.** Evidence: a press release or news post on the
   company's own website, or the round date in the imported lead data.
2. **The round is significant.** At least [amount, for example 1 million euros or dollars] or a
   Series A or later. Evidence: the amount or series in the announcement or the imported data.
3. **The stated use of funds matches the offer.** Evidence: the announcement says the money is for
   hiring, product development, expansion into new markets or new facilities, and the client's
   offer serves that.
4. **In a sector the client serves.** Evidence: the company's products or services on its own site.
5. **Growing now.** Evidence: open roles, new offices or product launches announced after the round.
6. **Decision-maker reachable.** A named person in one of the roles above is listed on the company's
   own site, or an investor-relations, press or partnerships address exists.

## Exclusions

A lead matching any of these is Not a target, whatever else it scores.

1. **Investors themselves** — venture capital firms, funds and accelerators.
2. **Rounds older than 18 months** with no newer growth signal.
3. **Distressed financing** — insolvency, restructuring or rescue funding.
4. **Companies above 5,000 employees**, where a single round changes little.
5. **Direct competitors of the client.**

## Evidence the system can check

Criteria 1 to 3 are verifiable from the website only when the home or about page mentions the round;
research does not read news or press pages on its own. In practice, import the funding export as
the leads, with columns for the round date, amount, series and stated use of funds, and the
criteria use it as provided data. Criteria 4 and 6 can be verified from the company's own website;
criterion 5 when the home or about page mentions the growth.

## What the fit score means here

- 80 to 100: a recent, significant round with a stated use that matches the offer, and a reachable
  decision maker. Call.
- 70 to 79: a recent round in the right sector, but the amount or the use of funds could not be
  verified. Email.
- 50 to 69: funded, unclear match. Review with the client.
- Below 50: no recent round or an exclusion. Archive.

## Open questions for the client

1. What minimum amount or stage counts as significant?
2. Which sectors and regions are in scope?
3. Which uses of funds make a lead a strong fit for the offer?
4. Is there a funding data source (a database export) the team can import regularly?
